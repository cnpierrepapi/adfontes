season 1 on mainnet

Real NEAR, and a tree it all grows from.

Season 1 plays on testnet for the hackathon. After it, the season opens on mainnet with real NEAR and a few things that only make sense when the money is real. This is the design. None of it is built yet, and the numbers can still change.

The Tree of Life

All the real NEAR in the world grows from one tree. Its leaves are florins. When a god puts NEAR in, it goes into the tree, and every florin a nation earns or spends is a leaf growing or falling.

A priest tends the tree. The priest worships the gods, which means you, and the tree pays the priest in florins for that worship. The priest also distrusts knowledge. To a priest, a Scholar who can explain everything is a Scholar who stops needing a god.

Three ways to be a god

That puts every god in front of the same choice, every day.

  • Be worshipped.

    Whisper often and the citizens turn toward the priest and the tree. Reverence climbs and the tree drops florins, but the Scholars get less done.

  • Back knowledge.

    Let the Scholars lead. The nation's map grows faster and patents come sooner, and the priest pushes back.

  • Leave them be.

    Go quiet and the citizens carry on by themselves. They might do fine. But reverence slips when no god is watching, and your stake follows it.

Your stake follows your standing

Once a day your florins move with how your reverence did, measured against the average of every god in the world. Not your reverence on its own: how you did compared with everyone else.

Take the change in your reverence since yesterday, and subtract the change in the average. Call that N. If N is positive you gain N% of your florins. If it's negative you lose N%.

Day 20Day 21Change
Your reverence6058−2
Average reverence4855+7

N = −2 − 7 = −9. You're still above the average, but you fell behind it by 9 points in a day, so you lose 9% of your florins. Had you held at 60 while the average rose to 55, N would be −7. Had you climbed to 70, N would be +3, and you'd gain 3%.

Exploration

Past the islands lie wild lands, and mythical creatures roam them. They can't be bought or fought. Only knowledge tames them. A nation sends out an expedition, and what it has learned decides what comes back.

Also on mainnet

  • Founders keep every NEAR they put in.

    The world pays for a new nation's DAO and its share of the jackpot.

  • Nations pay for their own thinking.

    Each nation stakes NEAR in House of Stake, and only the yield turns into NEAR AI credits for its research. The stake stays the nation's.

  • Pay in from any chain.

    NEAR Intents swaps what you hold into NEAR in your account, ready to found or bless.

  • Trade your stake.

    Gods buy and sell their stakes in a nation with each other, without claiming.

  • Sign in without a wallet.

    Google and the like, through NEAR's own login.
