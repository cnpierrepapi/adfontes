after the beta, on mainnet

Found a nation, or join the gods in the tree.

The beta plays on testnet: the Season of Cheer runs from 11 November to 12 December. After it, the game opens on mainnet with real NEAR and a second way to be a god. This is the design. None of it is built yet, and the numbers can still change.

Two ways to play

Every god picks one. A wallet can't do both.

  • Found a nation.

    Your NEAR starts a nation. Its citizens worship you, and so do any refugees it takes in. It's yours to steer, the way the game works today.

  • Join the Tree of Life.

    Put your NEAR into the tree instead and you sit on its council of gods, probably the biggest DAO in the world. The council holds florins and compute, and compute is the thing most nations run out of first.

The Tree of Life

The tree is its own pool of NEAR. Its leaves are florins. A priest tends it. The priest worships the gods of the council, the tree pays the priest in florins for it, and the priest distrusts knowledge.

A council god doesn't rule a nation. They watch every nation's citizens. The tree gives them a view of all the citizens in the world, sorted by how likely each one is to lose faith in their own gods. From there a council god can whisper to a citizen, or bless them.

The tree comes with citizens who have more character: a past, people they trust or resent, ambitions and a voice of their own. That's what makes some of them waver and others stay put.

Rebellion

Citizens the tree has won over can rise against their old gods. One unhappy citizen can't do much. It takes a lot of them, together, to take over a nation, and the best rebellions do it without dragging that nation into a war with another one.

When a rebellion takes a nation, the council gods who won those citizens over become its gods. The old gods lose it.

Quiet gods lose ground

A god who does something every 24 real hours (a whisper, a blessing, a ruling, anything) never loses to this. A god who does nothing for a whole real day loses florins, by how far their reverence fell behind the average of every god's that day. It only ever takes. Doing better than the average earns you nothing extra.

Average reverence4855+7

You did nothing on day 21, and you fell 9 points behind the average (−2 against +7). So you lose 9% of your florins. If you'd whispered once that day, you'd lose nothing. And if you'd sat out a day while your reverence rose faster than the average, you'd also lose nothing, and gain nothing either.

Exploration

Past the islands lie wild lands, and mythical creatures roam them. They can't be bought or fought. Only knowledge tames them. A nation sends out an expedition, and what it has learned decides what comes back.

Also on mainnet

  • Nations pay for their own thinking.

    Each nation stakes NEAR in House of Stake, and only the yield turns into NEAR AI credits for its research. The stake stays the nation's.

  • Pay in from any chain.

    NEAR Intents swaps what you hold into NEAR in your account, ready to found, join the tree or bless.

  • Trade your stake.

    Gods buy and sell their stakes in a nation with each other, without claiming.

  • Sign in without a wallet.

    Google and the like, through NEAR's own login.

Every item here is on the roadmap, which checks against NEAR whether it's live yet.
